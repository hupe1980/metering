German energy metering, correct by construction

A pure Rust domain library for the quantity calculations German metering requires — Europe/Berlin calendar arithmetic that knows a day is sometimes 23 hours and a Gastag starts at 06:00, Zählerstandsgang to Lastgang, gas m³→kWh_Hs and the SigLinDe gas SLP, Ersatzwertbildung in the market's own code list, check-digit-validated MaLo-IDs, and §14a Modul 3 tariff registers.

Three things it gets right that are easy to get wrong

A German day is not 24 hours

23 hours each spring, 25 each autumn. A completeness check built on a hard-coded 96 quarter-hours raises a false alarm every March and hides a real four-interval gap every October, because 96 of an expected 100 looks complete.

Time and the calendar →

The meter counts, it does not measure intervals

Since June 2025 the gateway delivers a Zählerstandsgang and the Messstellenbetreiber differences it into the Lastgang. Register wraps, implausible jumps and meter exchanges all live at that boundary.

Every citation is checked

Every German passage is matched against the published PDF, character for character, by a harness in the repository. Where a claim cannot be verified — the 2025 SLP dynamisation is printed as an image — the library says so instead of asserting it.
