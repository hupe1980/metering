Virtual meters

§ 42b EnWG Gemeinschaftliche Gebäudeversorgung: constant and proportional PV allocation, and the two caps that apply.

A virtual meter derives one series from others.

Two caps, from two places

§ 42b Abs. 5 EnWG caps the pool:

die rechnerisch aufteilbare Strommenge [ist] begrenzt […] auf die Strommenge, die innerhalb eines 15-Minuten-Zeitintervalls in der Solaranlage erzeugt oder von allen teilnehmenden Letztverbrauchern verbraucht wird, je nachdem welche dieser Strommengen geringer ist.

The per-tenant cap — the max(0, …), so no tenant is credited more PV than they drew — is the Pos() operator of the BDEW Anwendungshilfe Solarpaket 1 (v1.0, 25.01.2024), not that sentence. Both bounds together are what the module enforces.

Intersection semantics

Only timestamps present in all required source series appear in the output. A gap in any one source propagates rather than silently producing a wrong total — which for a GGV allocation would mean crediting a tenant against generation that was never measured.

Source series must share a timestamp grid; resample first if they do not.
